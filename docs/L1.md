# Level-1: Final flux calculations

Level 1 is the final flux calculation with EddyPro, run with
[fluxrun](https://github.com/holukas/fluxrun), one run per setup period. Its *FLUXNET* output is
the input of the [post-processing chain](FPC.qmd). FP2026.1 contains it in full: 329 columns,
listed in the section "L1 fluxes IRGA72" of the `_INFO.md` file.

The Level-1 fluxes are not quality-filtered, and `FC` carries no storage term. For analyses, use
the columns of the post-processing chain (see [QCF](QCF.md) and [Level 4.1](L4.1.md)).

Notebook: [`99_IRGA72_L1_MERGED_2016-2026.09`](notebooks/20_L1_FLUXES/IRGA72/99_IRGA72_L1_MERGED_2016-2026.09.html)
merges the runs and checks the merged record.

::: {.callout-note title="IRGA75 in progress"}
The Level-1 fluxes of the open-path LI-7500 (`IRGA75`) are in progress for FP2026.2 and are not
part of FP2026.1. They are merged, before the self-heating correction of [Level 1.1](L1.1.md), in
[`99_IRGA75_L1_MERGED_noSHC_2004-2017_2019`](notebooks/20_L1_FLUXES/IRGA75/99_IRGA75_L1_MERGED_noSHC_2004-2017_2019.html).

## Settings and inputs

The EddyPro settings of all runs, and how they differ between runs, are on
[Level-1: EddyPro settings](L1_EddyPro_Settings.md).

- **Time lags.** The nominal lags and lag windows were chosen from the Level-0 runs; see the
  [IRGA72 lag table on Level 0](L0.md#tbl-irga72-openlags).
- **Biomet input.** Six meteorological variables (`TA`, `RH`, `PA`, `SW_IN`, `LW_IN`, `PPFD_IN`)
  are passed to EddyPro, exported from the meteo products. The runs up to `2025` read the
  2004-2025 export, the `2026_1` run the export by
  [`EDDYPRO_BIOMET_CH-LAE_2004-2026.09`](notebooks/10_METEO/40_EXPORTS/EDDYPRO_BIOMET_CH-LAE_2004-2026.09.html).
  EddyPro copies them into its output; the merge removes these copies. The meteorological columns
  of the dataset come from the [meteo products](Meteo_Data.md).
- **Calibration gas.** The CO2 concentrations of 14 December 2017 to 15 March 2019 were corrected
  before the runs (see [Raw Data](Raw_Data.md)).

## Merging the runs

Runs that meet at a year boundary overlap by 12 half-hours (31 December 19:15 to 1 January 00:45),
and their values differ there, by up to 31.6 W m-2 in `LE` at the 2019/2020 boundary. Each record
is taken whole from one run: before midnight from the earlier run, from midnight on from the later
one. Columns without any value in the merged record are removed.
