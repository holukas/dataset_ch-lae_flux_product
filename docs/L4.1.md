# Level-4.1: Gap-filling

The gaps of each USTAR scenario (`CUT_16`, `CUT_50`, `CUT_84`) are filled with XGBoost, the method
of this dataset, and with MDS, the standard method of FLUXNET, for comparison only. Use the XGBoost
series. The gap-filled series end in `_gfXG` and `_gfMDS`; their flags `FLAG_..._ISFILLED` are 0
where the flux was measured.

## Input

The gap-filling starts from the records that passed Levels 2 to 3.3 with a quality flag of 0 or 1,
by day and by night (`NEE_L3.1_L3.3_<scenario>_QCF`, see [QCF](QCF.md)). The highest-quality
records, flag 0, are in `NEE_L3.1_L3.3_<scenario>_QCF0`; use them for analyses that need measured
values only. Both gas analysers use this selection. Gap-filling from flag 0 alone was tested and not
adopted: it leaves about a quarter of the records and raises the annual sums of both analysers.

The drivers are three complete meteo series at 47 m: air temperature
(`TA_T1_47_1_HOMOGENIZED_gfXG`), vapour pressure deficit (`VPD_T1_47_1_HOMOGENIZED`) and incoming
shortwave radiation (`SW_IN_T1_47_1_gfXG`). Temperature and VPD are the homogenised versions, which
correct for the probe changes in 2016 and June 2026.

## XGBoost

Each year has its own model for each scenario, trained on that year and its two neighbouring years,
with features engineered from the drivers and selected with SHAP values. All scenarios use the same
features. The number of trees is chosen once by cross-validation on held-out blocks of 14 days
(up to 1000 tested) and used for every year: 110 trees for `NEE`, 130 for `LE` and 140 for `H`. The
gaps are filled by a model refitted on all complete records. The 2016-2024 processing used a random
forest.

## MDS

MDS (`mode='diive'`) fills every gap, also long ones, and uses only measured driver values as
similar conditions. In long gaps it can rest on very few similar records, so a few unusual values
near the edge of a gap set the fill for days. The notebook splits the difference between the sums
of MDS and XGBoost by day and night, month and gap length.

## Long gaps without measurements

The model fills these gaps without measurements nearby:

- 1 to 22 January 2016, before and during the installation of the LI-7200. With the start-up
  period removed by hand (1 March to 25 May), 2016 lacks about 108 days of measurements, including
  the leaf-out, so its annual sum rests largely on the model;
- 18 May to 7 June 2018, failed flow module;
- 10 January to 20 March 2019, both LI-7200 units defective, then the LI-7500 as a stand-in, whose
  fluxes are in progress for FP2026.2 and not part of FP2026.1;
- 18 December 2020 to 4 January 2021;
- 13 December 2021 to 7 February 2022, raw data that cannot be converted.

`LE` has the same gaps as `FC`, and the periods removed by hand add to them. `H` exists in several
periods without `FC` (the 2016 start-up, 18 May to 7 June 2018, 6 to 10 January 2019, 18 December
2020 to 4 January 2021); its long gaps are 10 January to 20 March 2019 and 13 December 2021 to
7 February 2022. `LE` and `H` have no USTAR scenarios, no storage term and no uncertainty columns.

## Uncertainty

The uncertainty of the `CUT_50` series of each method follows Pastorello et al. (2020), in
µmol m-2 s-1 at 1 sigma:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| `_RANDUNC` | Random uncertainty: spread of measured fluxes under similar conditions |
| `_USTARUNC` | USTAR uncertainty: half the difference between the `CUT_84` and `CUT_16` series |
| `_JOINTUNC` | Both combined: √(RANDUNC² + USTARUNC²) |

For annual sums the random part adds in quadrature, the USTAR part as half the difference between
the `CUT_84` and `CUT_16` sums. The difference between XGBoost and MDS is not part of the joint
uncertainty; the notebook reports it beside it.

## Known limitations

- **MDS in spring 2016.** The data from 1 March to 25 May 2016 are removed (start-up of the
  LI-7200, see [Level 3.2](L3.2.md)). MDS fills this gap from time windows with a median width of
  70 days and gives far more uptake than XGBoost: April and May 2016 sum to -235 gC m-2 with MDS
  against -113 gC m-2 with XGBoost (`CUT_50`). In the other years, April and May range from -30 to
  -195 gC m-2 with either method. Use the XGBoost series.
- **`H` from 10 January to 20 March 2019** is model output only: there is no Level-1 `H` in this
  period (see [Level 1](L1.md)).
- **`H` in 2024** is about a third lower than in the other years. This has not been checked yet.
- **`NEE` of the open-path LI-7500 (FP2026.2, 2004-2015)** may show too much uptake after
  gap-filling, because the analyser misses many wet half-hours. See [Issues](Issues.md).
- **`LE` and `ET` of the two gas analysers** differ in humid and cold conditions; the LI-7200 may
  be too low there. In FP2026.2 this makes part of the step at 1 January 2016 instrumental. See
  [Issues](Issues.md).
