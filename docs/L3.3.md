# Level-3.3: USTAR filtering

Level 3.3 flags the `NEE` records of low turbulence. A record with a friction velocity `USTAR`
below the threshold gets flag 2, by day and by night; unlike ONEFlux, the first record above the
threshold is kept. The filter is run at three constant thresholds, which give three USTAR
scenarios: `CUT_16`, `CUT_50` and `CUT_84`. The flags enter the overall quality flag
([QCF](QCF.md)) of each scenario.

Method, evidence and checks:

- [`01_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/01_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE_2016-2026.09.html),

- [diive: USTAR filter](https://diive.readthedocs.io/en/latest/flux_postprocessing.html#ustar-filter):

## Thresholds and records

| Scenario | Threshold (m s-1) | Removed by the USTAR filter | Records for gap-filling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CUT_16` | 0.272 | 31,447 (17 %) | 92,412 (49 %) |
| `CUT_50` | 0.304 | 37,102 (20 %) | 86,757 (46 %) |
| `CUT_84` | 0.340 | 43,610 (23 %) | 80,249 (43 %) |

: USTAR thresholds and `NEE` records per scenario, as a share of all 188,448 half-hours of
FP2026.1. Records already removed by Levels 2 to 3.2 (51,004, 27 %) are not counted as removed by
the USTAR filter. {#tbl-l33-scenarios}

`CUT_50` is the best estimate. `CUT_16` and `CUT_84` bound it: the lower threshold keeps more
records of low turbulence, the higher one removes more. Their difference gives the USTAR
uncertainty of [Level 4.1](L4.1.md).

The same three thresholds are used for every year. They are kept on purpose, although the
thresholds that FLUXNET uses for CH-Lae are much higher.

## Columns

For each scenario `<CUT>`:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| `FLAG_L3.3_<CUT>_NEE_L3.1_USTAR_TEST` | USTAR filter: 0 kept, 2 removed |
| `FLAG_L3.3_<CUT>_NEE_L3.1_QCF` | Overall quality flag after all tests of Levels 2 to 3.3 |
| `NEE_L3.1_L3.3_<CUT>_QCF` | `NEE` with quality flag 0 or 1, the input of the gap-filling |
| `NEE_L3.1_L3.3_<CUT>_QCF0` | `NEE` with quality flag 0 only |

: Level-3.3 columns of `NEE`. {#tbl-l33-columns}

## Latent and sensible heat flux

`LE` (and therefore `ET`) and `H` are not filtered for USTAR, following Pastorello et al. (2020):
it is not established that the advection that biases the CO2 flux at low turbulence also affects
the energy fluxes, which are small at night when advection is largest. Their Level-3.3 columns
carry `CUT_NONE` in the name, for example `LE_L3.1_L3.3_CUT_NONE_QCF`.

## Threshold detection

The thresholds were detected with the R package REddyProc (`usControlUstarEst` settings,
100 bootstrap runs), following Pastorello et al. (2020). The detection used nighttime `NEE` after
Level 3.2 and was run separately for the two gas analysers. The 16th, 50th and 84th percentiles
of the bootstrap results give the three scenarios.

| Analyser | Period | Nighttime records used | REddyProc settings changed from default | `CUT_16` | `CUT_50` | `CUT_84` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LI-7200 (`IRGA72`) | 2016-2024 | QCF 0 and 1 | `ustPlateauFwd = 9` (10), `ustPlateauBack = 5` (6), `UstarClasses = 30` (20) | 0.271620 | 0.301811 | 0.333478 |
| LI-7500 (`IRGA75`) | 2005-2017, 2019 | QCF 0 | `ustPlateauFwd = 8` (10), `ustPlateauBack = 4` (6), `UstarClasses = 30` (20) | 0.268384 | 0.303628 | 0.339684 |
| **Used** | | | | **0.271620** | **0.303628** | **0.339684** |

: USTAR thresholds (m s-1) detected for the two analysers, and the thresholds used. For each
scenario the higher of the two is used. Defaults in brackets. {#tbl-l33-detection}

The LI-7200 detection included records of QCF 1, because with QCF 0 only the plateau of nighttime
`NEE` against `USTAR` was not detected as well.
