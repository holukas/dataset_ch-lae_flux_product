# Level-2: Quality flag expansion

Level 2 turns the quality information of the EddyPro output into one flag per test,
`FLAG_L2_FC_<TEST>_TEST` (0 passed, 1 warning, 2 rejected), and combines them into the overall flag
`FLAG_L2_FC_QCF` (see [QCF](QCF.md)). The tests are the same as in the 2016-2024 processing.

Notebook: [`05_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/05_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE_2016-2026.09.html),
Step 2.

| Flag | Test | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| `SSITC` | Steady state and developed turbulence (EddyPro) | Flag as reported |
| `SCF` | Size of the spectral correction factor | Warning from 2, rejected from 4 |
| `SIGNAL_STRENGTH` | Signal strength of the LI-7200 (`CUSTOM_SIGNAL_STRENGTH_IRGA72_MEAN`) | Rejected below 60 |
| `CO2_VM97_SPIKE_HF` | Spikes in the raw data (Vickers and Mahrt 1997) | Hard flag |
| `CO2_VM97_DROPOUT_HF` | Dropouts in the raw data (Vickers and Mahrt 1997) | Hard flag |

: Level-2 tests of `FC`. {#tbl-l2-tests}

Gas completeness, angle of attack and steadiness of the horizontal wind are not tested. In
practice the SSITC test rejects nearly all records that Level 2 removes.

## Latent and sensible heat flux

Notebooks: [`25_FPPC_IRGA72_LE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/25_FPPC_IRGA72_LE_2016-2026.09.html),
[`35_FPPC_IRGA72_H`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/35_FPPC_IRGA72_H_2016-2026.09.html).

- **`LE`** uses the same tests, with its own SSITC and spectral correction flags and the raw-data
  tests of H2O.
- **`H`** comes from the sonic anemometer alone, so the tests of the gas analyser (signal strength,
  raw-data tests of CO2 and H2O) do not apply. The raw-data tests are those of the sonic
  temperature (`T_SONIC_VM97_TEST`): spikes, dropouts and absolute limits. The absolute-limits test
  is added for `H`, because a sonic temperature outside its plausible range indicates water or ice
  on the transducers.
