# Level-3.2: Outlier flagging

Five tests remove outliers from `NEE_L3.1`, six for the open-path LI-7500 (`IRGA75`). Each writes a
flag column `FLAG_L3.2_NEE_L3.1_QCF_OUTLIER_<TEST>_TEST` (0 kept, 2 removed); a record counts for
the first test that removed it.

Method, evidence and checks:

- [`05_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/05_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE_2016-2026.09.html)
  and [`05_FPPC_IRGA75_NEE_OPCO`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA75/05_FPPC_IRGA75_NEE_OPCO_2004-2017.html),
  Step 4: one plot per test and an overview of all removals.
- [diive: outlier tests](https://diive.readthedocs.io/en/latest/flux_postprocessing.html#outlier-tests):
  how the tests work.

## Tests

| Flag | Test | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| `MANUAL` | Periods removed by hand, see below | Whole days, both ends included |
| `ABSLIM` | Absolute limits | ±50 µmol m-2 s-1 |
| `HAMPEL` | Hampel filter on double differences: single spikes | 13 days, 5.5 sigma, day and night separately |
| `HAMPEL_2` | Hampel filter on the values per time of day: values wrong for their time of day | 13 days, 5.5 sigma, the half-hour and one on either side, at least 10 values |
| `NEGNIGHT` | Nighttime values clearly below zero | Below −4 times the robust spread of all nighttime values within ±15 days |
| `NEGNIGHTS` | Whole nights clearly below zero, open-path LI-7500 (`IRGA75`, FP2026.2) only | Night median below −2 standard errors of that median (√(π/2) × the same spread / √n), nights with at least 6 values |

The test of whole nights catches nights of apparent uptake that the per-record test lets through,
because each record lies within a few spreads of zero. They occur with the LI-7500 in fog and rime,
when its windows are wet or iced: below 5 °C its nighttime `NEE` falls to a median of −0.1
µmol m-2 s-1 at a relative humidity above 99.5 %, while the LI-7200 keeps +2.4 in the same
conditions. On the LI-7500 data the test removes about 45 nights.

The nighttime test uses the spread of all nighttime values rather than the uncertainty of each
record. The per-record uncertainty grows with bad values in a noisy stretch: on 12 December 2021,
while the LI-7200 was failing, night values of −41, −20 and −13 µmol m-2 s-1 passed a test based on
it. High nighttime values are real respiration and are not tested.

Compared with the 2016-2024 processing, the absolute limits are kept, and the trimming of low
values and the local standard deviation test are replaced by the two Hampel filters and the
nighttime test.

## Instrument problems and periods removed by hand

- **1 to 15 December 2004, open-path LI-7500 (`IRGA75`, FP2026.2).** Fog and rime at about −2 °C,
  97 to 99 % relative humidity and little wind: nighttime `NEE` of −10 to −20 µmol m-2 s-1 with a
  positive nighttime sensible heat flux, and from 10 December an AGC of 94. The values passed the
  automatic tests because the error lasted for days. Removed by hand.
- **16 to 23 November 2013, 9 to 12 November 2014 and 24 November to 7 December 2014, `IRGA75`.**
  Fog and rime as in December 2004, at 99 % relative humidity: nighttime `NEE` down to −17
  µmol m-2 s-1, erratic `LE`, and AGC values up to 94. Removed by hand.
- **Start-up of the LI-7200 in 2016.** During the installation (11 to 22 January) and a period with
  too low a flow rate (1 March to 25 May), water in the filter lowered the fluxes. Both periods are
  removed by hand.
- **19 May to 7 June 2018.** There is no `FC`: the flow module of the analyser had failed, so this
  period was processed with the sonic anemometer only.
- **30 December 2018 to 10 January 2019.** The LI-7200 (IRGA72-B) was failing: low signal strength
  from December, an iced inlet filter, error codes and repeated stops of the data acquisition. It
  was replaced on 11 January 2019. Removed by hand.
- **8 November to 6 December 2020.** A fault of the gas analyser: the scatter of the flux rose to
  many times its usual level, with half-hourly values from −190 to +190 µmol m-2 s-1, while the flow
  rate and the sonic stayed normal. The signal strength fell from 100 to about 60 on 12 November and
  recovered after the LI-7200 was replaced on 10 December. 8 to 27 November and 4 to 6 December are
  removed by hand; the days between them are within the usual scatter.
- **15 January to 4 February 2026.** The nighttime scatter was about twice its seasonal level, also
  after the outlier tests, with winter daytime values above 20 µmol m-2 s-1. The signal strength fell
  from 93 to 87; the scatter ended when the LI-7200 was cleaned on 4 February. Removed by hand.
- **Wrong calibration gas, 14 December 2017 to 15 March 2019.** The CO2 concentrations of this
  period were calibrated against the wrong reference gas. The correction, a factor of 0.974 on the
  concentration, was applied when the raw data were converted with bico, so `FC` is corrected.

The long gaps without any `FC` are listed on [Level-4.1](L4.1.md).

## Latent and sensible heat flux

The notebooks [`25_FPPC_IRGA72_LE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/25_FPPC_IRGA72_LE_2016-2026.09.html)
and [`35_FPPC_IRGA72_H`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/35_FPPC_IRGA72_H_2016-2026.09.html),
and for the LI-7500 [`25_FPPC_IRGA75_LE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA75/25_FPPC_IRGA75_LE_2004-2017.html)
and [`35_FPPC_IRGA75_H`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA75/35_FPPC_IRGA75_H_2004-2017.html),
run the first four tests. Negative nighttime values are not tested:
`LE` can be negative at night (dew), and a negative `H` at night is physical.

| Flux | Absolute limits | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `LE` | −100 to 1000 W m-2 | Wide on purpose, the same for both gas analysers: the limits remove only values far outside the possible range, and the Hampel filters that follow decide on the rest. |
| `H` | −200 to 800 W m-2 | The upper limit keeps the largest daytime values that agree with the incoming radiation, in spring before leaf-out; the lower limit lies below the nighttime values of summer and removes the large negative values of wet and freezing episodes in winter. |

Periods removed by hand:

- **`LE`**: the same windows as `FC`. The LI-7200 measures H2O and CO2 in the same cell, so its
  faults affect both fluxes; each window was checked in `LE`.
- **`H`** comes from the sonic anemometer alone, so the faults of the gas analyser do not apply: `H`
  stays normal during the 2016 start-up and the November 2020 fault. In the LI-7200 years only
  **20 to 26 December 2018** is removed: after work at the LI-7200 the instrument boom was not fixed in its position, so the
  sonic could move with it.
- **1 to 15 December 2004, open-path LI-7500 (`IRGA75`, FP2026.2).** The fog and rime episode
  removed from `FC` is also removed from `LE` and `H`: nighttime `LE` fell to −20 to −50 W m-2,
  daytime `LE` turned negative, and nighttime `H` rose to +7 to +24 W m-2. The sonic anemometer is
  shared, so rime on it affects all three fluxes. The same holds for the three fog episodes of
  2013 and 2014, which are removed from `LE` and `H` as well.
- **Sonic electronics, 19 December 2019 to 10 January 2020.** After the change from the HS-100 to
  the HS-50 (see [Instrumentation](Instrumentation.md)) the electronics unit of the HS-100 stayed in
  use, so the fieldbook rates these data as possibly not entirely accurate. `H` of this period does
  not differ from the weeks around it and is kept.
- **Wet or freezing weather.** The sonic can give `H` of several hundred W m-2 of either sign, while
  the sonic temperature falls several kelvin below its usual offset from the air temperature: water
  or ice on the transducers. These episodes occur every winter, most often in 2016 to 2019, before
  the sonic head was replaced. The absolute limits and the Hampel filters remove their values.
