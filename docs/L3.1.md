# Level-3.1: Storage correction

The storage term is added to the CO2 flux: `NEE_L3.1` is `FC` plus the single-point storage term
`SC_SINGLE` from EddyPro. The storage term used is in `SC_SINGLE_gfRMED_L3.1`, and every record with
a measured `FC` has one. The same steps apply to both gas analysers; for the open-path LI-7500
(`IRGA75`, in progress for FP2026.2) `FC` is the flux corrected at [Level 1.1](L1.1.md). The
examples below are from the LI-7200 (`IRGA72`).

`LE` and `H` get no storage term, as in Pastorello et al. (2020). Their Level-3.1 columns
`LE_L3.1` and `H_L3.1` keep the level in the name but equal `LE` and `H`.

## Screening

The single-point storage term is the change in the CO2 concentration at 47 m between two
consecutive records, so errors in the concentration enter it directly. Two steps screen it before it is
added; the values they remove are filled with a rolling median.

- **Next to gaps in the concentration.** After a signal loss the LI-7200 can restart with readings
  far from the real concentration and return to it over hours, for example 695 ppm on
  21 December 2017. The return entered the storage term as a large negative flux, while the jump
  at the restart fell into the gap and was never counted. Over 2016 to 2026 these one-sided
  changes summed to about −150 gC m-2. Next to each gap in `CO2_MOLAR_DENSITY`, the storage values
  are discarded until the concentration is back within 3 robust standard deviations of its median
  at the same time of day (±7 days).
- **Spikes.** Isolated spikes, found with a Hampel filter on the double differences, are replaced
  together with one record on either side by the mean of that block. The block keeps its sum, so
  the storage term stays balanced.

After the screening the cumulative storage term at measured fluxes stays close to zero, except in
December 2017 to January 2018 and in November 2020, when the analyser was failing. The fluxes of
these periods are rejected by the signal strength test or removed by hand at Level 3.2, so their
storage term does not enter the flux. The numbers of each run are in the notebook.

## Columns

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| `NEE_L3.1` | `FC` plus the storage term, µmol m-2 s-1 |
| `SC_SINGLE_gfRMED_L3.1` | Storage term used, µmol m-2 s-1 |
| `FLAG_SC_SINGLE_gfRMED_L3.1_ISFILLED` | 0 measured, 1 filled because missing, 2 spike block replaced by its mean, 3 discarded next to a gap and filled |
