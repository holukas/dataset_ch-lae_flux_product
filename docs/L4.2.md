# Level-4.2: NEE Partitioning

Partitioning of NEE into GPP and ecosystem respiration is **not part of FP2026.1**
(`RUN_PARTITIONING = False` in
[`01_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/01_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE_2016-2026.09.html)).

When it is switched on, the notebook splits the flux of each USTAR scenario with the nighttime
method (Reichstein et al. 2005) and the daytime method (Lasslop et al. 2010) as ONEFlux implements
them, on the flux gap-filled with XGBoost. ONEFlux itself partitions a flux filled with MDS, so the
results are not identical to ONEFlux output. See
[diive: partitioning](https://diive.readthedocs.io/en/latest/flux_postprocessing.html#partitioning).
