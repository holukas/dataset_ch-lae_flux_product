# Level-4.2: NEE partitioning

The partitioning of `NEE` into gross primary production (`GPP`) and ecosystem respiration
(`RECO`) is **not part of FP2026.1**. For FP2026.2 it is switched on (`RUN_PARTITIONING = True`) in
[`05_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/05_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE_2016-2026.09.html)
and [`05_FPPC_IRGA75_NEE_OPCO`](notebooks/30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA75/05_FPPC_IRGA75_NEE_OPCO_2004-2017.html),
Step 8.

The notebooks partition the `NEE` of each USTAR scenario, gap-filled with XGBoost from the records
with `QCF` 0 and 1, with the nighttime method (Reichstein et al. 2005) and the daytime method
(Lasslop et al. 2010) as implemented in ONEFlux. ONEFlux itself partitions a flux gap-filled with
MDS, so the results differ from ONEFlux output. See
[diive: partitioning](https://diive.readthedocs.io/en/latest/flux_postprocessing.html#partitioning).

The nighttime method fits `RECO` on measured `NEE` only. For the open-path LI-7500 (`IRGA75`), which
loses many wet nights, this biases `RECO` and `GPP` low. See [Issues](Issues.md).

The daytime method fits its light response only on measured shortwave radiation, which starts on
14 September 2005. Before that date it cannot fit, so its columns (`_DT_`) are empty from 2004 to
14 September 2005 11:15. The nighttime method does not need radiation and covers the whole record. `GPP` and `RECO` of
the early LI-7500 years are compared with the LI-7200 years and with independent estimates on
[Comparisons of the LI-7500 years](IRGA75_Comparisons.md).
