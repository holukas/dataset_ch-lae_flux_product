# Level-0: Preliminary flux calculations

Level 0 is a preliminary flux calculation with relaxed settings. Its results are not part of the
dataset. They are used to choose the settings of the final calculation ([Level 1](L1.md)):

- to check that the flux processing works,
- to check the wind direction across years,
- to choose the time-lag windows for the final runs.

The Level-0 output of all EddyPro runs is merged into one record per gas analyser, and the checks
below read that record. The method is described in the
[Swiss FluxNet flux processing chain](https://www.swissfluxnet.ethz.ch/index.php/data/ecosystem-fluxes/flux-processing-chain/#Step_2_Level-0_Preliminary_Flux_Calculations_With_OPENLAG_and_Other_Tests).

::: {.callout-note title="IRGA75 in progress"}
FP2026.1 contains the fluxes of the enclosed-path LI-7200 (`IRGA72`) only. The Level-0 results of
the open-path LI-7500 (`IRGA75`) are shown here because they set up its final runs, which are in
progress for FP2026.2. The `IRGA75` fluxes are not part of FP2026.1.
:::

## Time lags from OPENLAG runs

::: {.callout-tip title="Notebooks: time lag checks"}
- `IRGA72`: [05_IRGA72-L0_check_timelags_2016-2026.09](notebooks/00_L0_CHECKS/IRGA72/05_IRGA72-L0_check_timelags_2016-2026.09.html)
- `IRGA75`: [15_IRGA75-L0_check_timelags](notebooks/00_L0_CHECKS/IRGA75/15_IRGA75-L0_check_timelags.html)
:::

The lag between the turbulent fluctuations of the vertical wind and of the gas was first searched
in a wide time window (OPENLAG). For each setup period, the peak of the histogram of the lags found
became the nominal (default) lag, and a window as narrow as possible around it became the lag
search window of the final runs.

### IRGA72 (2016 to September 2026)

- The OPENLAG window was `-1 s` to `+10 s` from 2016 to 2018 and `-1 s` to `+20 s` from 2019 on.
  The wider window was needed because an artificial lag of about 8 s was introduced in 2019.
- The window starts at `-1 s` rather than `0 s` because EddyPro started the search at a later lag,
  for example `+1 s`, when the start value was `0 s`.

## Wind direction across years

::: {.callout-tip title="Notebooks: wind direction checks"}
- `IRGA72`: [04_IRGA72-L0_check_winddir_2016-2026.09](notebooks/00_L0_CHECKS/IRGA72/04_IRGA72-L0_check_winddir_2016-2026.09.html)
- `IRGA75`: [14_IRGA75-L0_check_winddir](notebooks/00_L0_CHECKS/IRGA75/14_IRGA75-L0_check_winddir.html)
:::

The wind directions were calculated with a sonic orientation of 209° to north. For each year, a
histogram of the half-hourly wind directions (bin width 1°) was compared with that of a reference
period. The offset is the number of degrees to add to the wind directions of a year to make its
histogram most similar to the reference. All offsets are within ±3°, which indicates that the
sonic orientation stayed close to 209° in all years.
