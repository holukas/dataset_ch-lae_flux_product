Level-1: EddyPro settings

The Level-1 fluxes of FP2026.1 come from 18 EddyPro runs, one per setup period of the enclosed-path LI-7200 (IRGA72). All belong to the Level-1 version HS50_IRGA72_FF-202610. This page lists the settings of these runs, taken from their EddyPro project (.eddypro) and metadata (.metadata) files and checked against the EddyPro output. The runs and the merged record are described on Level-1; the setup periods on Yearly Notes.

The processing settings are the same in all 18 runs. What differs between runs are the gas analyser, its position, the time-lag windows, the source of the air temperature and whether a run computes its own spectral assessment.

Settings common to all runs

Settings that differ between runs

  • Time lags. The lags of about 8.4 s from 2019_4 on are not the travel time through the intake tube. According to the setup notes 12 and 13 (see Yearly Notes), they include an artificial delay added by the data acquisition. 2022_2 has no such delay, and its CO2 lag of 1.25 s lies in the expected range. The lag shifts again in 2022_3 and 2022_4, which are therefore separate runs (note 7). Lags are not comparable across setup periods.
  • Analyser position. The inlet was moved in March 2023 (note 23), which changes the separation from 2023_2 on. Tube length and flow are unchanged.
  • Air temperature. Up to 2018_1 the air temperature comes from the LI-7500, which is in the same raw files. From 2018_2 on the LI-7500 is no longer in the raw files, and the air temperature comes from the biomet file.
  • Biomet file. The runs up to 2025_1 read EDDYPRO_BIOMET_CH-LAE_2004-2025.csv, the 2026_1 run reads EDDYPRO_BIOMET_CH-LAE_2004-2026.09.csv. The file holds TA, RH, SW_IN, LW_IN, PA and PPFD_IN at the 47 m tower level, with the timestamp at the end of the half-hour. Over 2004 to 2025 the two files differ only in model fills, reconstructions and one half-hour on 16 May 2025.

Runs

Each run folder holds its project and metadata file, named after the setup period and the instruments, for example CH-LAE_2025_1_[IRGA72]_HS50-B_IRGA72-A.eddypro.

Known limitations

  • Sonic model. All runs declare a Gill HS-50. From 2016 to 19 December 2019 the sonic was a Gill HS-100 (fieldbook). EddyPro uses the model for the path lengths of the sonic, so the HS-50 entry is nominal for that period.
  • Gaps between runs. The setup periods 2019_2, 2019_3, 2021_2 and 2022_1 have no IRGA72 run; see Level-1.
