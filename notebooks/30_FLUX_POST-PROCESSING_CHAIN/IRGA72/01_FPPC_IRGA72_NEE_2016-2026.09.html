ℹ️ About this notebook

Processes a CO2 flux with the diive flux post-processing chain: storage term (L3.1), outliers (L3.2), USTAR filter (L3.3), gap-filling (L4.1), partitioning (L4.2) and uncertainty. The site, flux, period and all settings are in Settings; Data sources links the site documentation, which holds the decisions and periods specific to the site.

TEST_RUN = True runs one year (TEST_YEAR) and writes its own output file.

📇 Data sources

The input 30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/00_FPPC_IRGA72_INPUT_2016-2026.09.parquet in the external data folder holds the Level-1 fluxes and the meteo data of the site.

Site documentation (CH-LAE): the processing levels, settings, periods removed by hand and known limitations are on the CH-LAE flux product site, Levels 3.1 to 4.2.

Step 3: storage term (Level 3.1)

Adds the storage term to the flux (FC + SC_SINGLE), after two screening steps:

  • Next to gaps in CO2_CONC: after a signal loss an analyser can restart far from the real concentration, and its return would count as a one-sided storage flux. The storage values are discarded until the concentration is normal again for its time of day.
  • Spikes: each isolated spike, with one record on either side, is replaced by the block mean, which keeps the sum.

The removed values are filled with a rolling median, so every measured flux keeps a storage term. Details: diive.

def period_sums(frame, freq):
    """Sums per period and their 1-sigma uncertainty: the random part in quadrature, the USTAR
    part as half the spread of the CUT_84 and CUT_16 sums."""
    s = frame.resample(freq).sum()
    out = pd.DataFrame({'sum': s['flux'], 'random': np.sqrt(s['var']),
                        'USTAR': ((s['upper'] - s['lower']) / 2).abs()})
    out['joint'] = np.sqrt(out['random'] ** 2 + out['USTAR'] ** 2)
    return out
