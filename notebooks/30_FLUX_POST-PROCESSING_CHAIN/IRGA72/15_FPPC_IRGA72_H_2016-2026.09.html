Flux post-processing chain: sensible heat flux H (2016-2026.09)

Modified

27 October 2026

notebook version: 1 (26 Oct 2026). Built from the CO2 flux notebook 01_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE_2016-2026.09.ipynb in this folder, which is adapted from the diive notebook FluxPostProcessingChain.ipynb.

ℹ️ About this notebook

Processes the sensible heat flux H with the diive flux post-processing chain: quality flags (L2), outliers (L3.2) and gap-filling with XGBoost and MDS (L4.1). As in FLUXNET2015 and ONEFlux (Pastorello et al. 2020), H has no storage term (L3.1) and no USTAR filter (L3.3), and there is no partitioning, uncertainty step or energy balance closure correction. The site, flux, period and all settings are in Settings; Data sources links the site documentation, which holds the decisions and periods specific to the site.

TEST_RUN = True runs one year (TEST_YEAR) and writes its own output file.

📇 Data sources

The input 30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/00_FPPC_IRGA72_INPUT_2016-2026.09.parquet in the external data folder holds the Level-1 fluxes and the meteo data of the site.

Site documentation (CH-LAE): the processing levels, settings, periods removed by hand and known limitations are on the CH-LAE flux product site, Levels 3.1 to 4.1.

        # Day 366 exists only in leap years, so the band stops before it.
        _ref = _ref.loc[_ref.index < 366]
        # The legend gives the mean and SD of the annual totals of the full years.
        _totals = [_cum[_y].dropna().iloc[-1] for _y in _years if _y not in (_partial or [])]
        _end_mean, _end_sd = np.mean(_totals), np.std(_totals, ddof=1)
        _ax.fill_between(_ref.index, _ref['MEAN-SD'], _ref['MEAN+SD'], color=cp.KEPT,
                         alpha=0.3, linewidth=0, label=f'mean ± 1 SD: ±{_end_sd:,.0f}')
        _ax.plot(_ref.index, _ref['MEAN_DOY_TIME'], color=cp.INK, linewidth=1.2,
                 linestyle=(0, (4, 3)), label=f'mean: {_end_mean:,.0f}')
    for _shade, _year in zip(_shades, _years):
        _valid = _cum[_year].dropna()
        _ax.plot(_cum.index, _cum[_year], color=_shade, linewidth=1.5,
                 label=f"{_year}: {_valid.iloc[-1]:,.0f}")
    _ax.axhline(0, color=cp.INK, linewidth=0.8, alpha=0.5)
    cp.style_axes(_ax)
    cp.panel_label(_ax, _names.get(_method, _method))
    _ax.set_xticks(_months, ['J', 'F', 'M', 'A', 'M', 'J', 'J', 'A', 'S', 'O', 'N', 'D'])
    _ax.set_xlim(1, 367)
    _ax.legend(frameon=False, fontsize=8, ncol=1 if len(_years) < 8 else 2)
