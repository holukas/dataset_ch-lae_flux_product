Flux post-processing chain: IRGA72 latent heat flux (2016-2026.09)

Modified

27 October 2026

notebook version: 1 (26 Oct 2026). Built from the CO2-flux notebook of this folder (01_FPPC_IRGA72_NEE_2016-2026.09), which is adapted from the diive notebook FluxPostProcessingChain.ipynb.

ℹ️ About this notebook

Processes a latent heat flux with the diive flux post-processing chain: outliers (L3.2), gap-filling (L4.1) and the conversion to evapotranspiration (ET). As in FLUXNET2015 and ONEFlux (Pastorello et al. 2020), LE gets no storage term (L3.1) and no USTAR filter (L3.3); both levels run, because the chain needs them. The site, flux, period and all settings are in Settings; Data sources links the site documentation, which holds the decisions and periods specific to the site.

TEST_RUN = True runs one year (TEST_YEAR) and writes its own output file.

📇 Data sources

The input 30_FLUX_POST-PROCESSING_CHAIN/IRGA72/00_FPPC_IRGA72_INPUT_2016-2026.09.parquet in the external data folder holds the Level-1 fluxes and the meteo data of the site.

Site documentation (CH-LAE): the processing levels, settings, periods removed by hand and known limitations are on the CH-LAE flux product site, Levels 3.1 to 4.2.

Step 11: evapotranspiration

ET is LE divided by the latent heat of vaporization, which depends on air temperature (et_from_le with TA_F, after the R package bigleaf). The ET column of each gap-filling method holds mm per half-hour and sums directly to totals. The input column ET is the EddyPro rate of the measured records only.
