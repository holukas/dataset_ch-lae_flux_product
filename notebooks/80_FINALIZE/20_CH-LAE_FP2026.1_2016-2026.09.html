ℹ️ About this notebook

This notebook assembles the dataset of the release FP2026.1, CH-LAE_FP2026.1_2016-2026.09. It covers 1 January 2016 to 30 September 2026 and is cut from the collection 10_FLUXES_and_METEO_2004-2026.09, which holds all data available so far on one 30-minute timestamp from 2004 on.

The cut keeps the records of the release period and the variables that have at least one value in it. Variables whose record ends before 2016 are therefore not part of the release.

The dataset is written as parquet and as CSV. The last section lists every variable of the dataset with its unit, a short description, its source and its period. The units and descriptions come from variable_info.py in this folder.
