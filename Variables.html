Variables

The main variables of FP2026.1: column name, unit and meaning. All are half-hourly. The index TIMESTAMP_MIDDLE marks the middle of each half-hour, in UTC+01:00 all year (no daylight saving). The _INFO.md file that comes with the dataset lists all 548 columns with unit and description; this page covers the columns most users need.

Fluxes

The fluxes come from the enclosed-path LI-7200 (IRGA72). The column names follow the processing levels of the post-processing chain. For example, NEE_L3.1_L3.3_CUT_50_QCF_gfXG is NEE after Level 3.1 (storage term) and Level 3.3 (USTAR filter, scenario CUT_50), from records with quality flag 0 or 1 (QCF), gap-filled with XGBoost (gfXG).

Each gap-filled column also exists with _gfMDS instead of _gfXG, filled with MDS. These are for comparison only; use the XGBoost columns. The three USTAR thresholds are 0.272, 0.304 and 0.340 m s-1 (Level 3.3).

The other Level-1 columns are the EddyPro output: the fluxes after each correction stage, the quality tests, wind and turbulence statistics, footprint distances and raw-data statistics.

NoteTwo units for ET

The gap-filled ET_..._gfXG and ET_..._gfMDS are in mm per half-hour. The Level-1 column ET from EddyPro is in mm h-1.

Meteorological variables

Measured at the tower at 47 m (T1_47_1) or in the soil profile at the forest floor (FF1_<depth>, depth in metres). Each variable has its own page with its columns, flags, coverage and known limitations. The provenance flags (FLAG_<column>_ISFILLED, _SOURCE and others) are defined there.

Column suffixes: _HOMOGENIZED is corrected for sensor changes, so the record is consistent over time; _gfXG and _GAPFILLED are gap-filled. PREC, SWC, TS, G, SW_OUT and LW_OUT end on 31 December 2025 and are empty in 2026.
